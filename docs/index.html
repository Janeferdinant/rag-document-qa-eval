RAG document QA

Ask a question over three Wikipedia articles. Retrieval (BM25) runs in your browser and the retrieved passages are shown with numbered citations next to the answer.

Before you use a key

  • Your key stays in this tab's memory. It is not saved (no cookies, no local storage), not logged, and sent only to Google's Gemini API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com), never to this site.
  • Calls are billed to your own Google account or use its free quota. Google's terms may require a paid service in some regions. Gemini API terms, get a key.
  • Passages from the sample corpus and your question are sent to Google with your key. Do not type confidential information.

Mode

Mock mode: precomputed examples, no key

Ask

Without a key you get the retrieved passages only. For an answer, pick a precomputed example or add your key.
